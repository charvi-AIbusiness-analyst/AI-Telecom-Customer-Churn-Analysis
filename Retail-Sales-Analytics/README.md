# 📌 Project Overview

Retail Sales Performance Analytics is an end-to-end AI Business Analyst portfolio project that analyzes retail sales data to uncover business trends, identify performance gaps, and provide executive-level recommendations for increasing revenue and profitability.

This project follows a complete Business Analysis lifecycle including requirement gathering, KPI design, SQL analysis, Power BI dashboard development, and AI-assisted business recommendations.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Retail companies generate large volumes of sales data every day.

However, management struggles to answer questions like:

- Which products generate maximum profit?
- Which regions underperform?
- Which customer segments contribute the highest revenue?
- Which categories require improvement?
- How can sales be increased?

This project answers these questions using Business Analysis, SQL, Power BI, and AI.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze retail sales performance
- Identify profitable products
- Discover low-performing regions
- Evaluate customer purchasing behavior
- Build Executive Dashboard
- Improve business decision making
- Recommend AI-driven business strategies

# 🔍 Business Insights

- High revenue comes from a limited number of products.
- Certain regions consistently outperform others.
- Some categories generate high revenue but low profit.
- Customer purchasing behavior varies by location.
- Seasonal sales trends influence revenue.
